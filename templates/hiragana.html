How to learn.

  1. Learn the characters in order
    (あ→ん).
  2. Say the sound out loud.
  3. Prectice writing them every day.
  4. Review daily to remember them.

What you'll learn.

  • Recognize all 46 basic hiragana characters.
  • Recognize all 20 Dakuten Hiragana.
  • Say all correct pronunciation for each character.
  • Read simple words and short phrases.
