Learn Hiragana

Hiragana is one of the basic writing systems used in Japanese.
Here you will learn the characters and their sounds. Take your time
and practice a little each day.

How to learn.

  1. 1 Learn the characters in order
    (あ→ん).
  2. 2 Say the sound out loud.
  3. 3 Prectice writing them every day.
